## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an elevated lifestyle and holistic performance aesthetic designed for thoughtful daily rituals. The design merges the focused rigor of neo-fintech interfaces with the serene, ambient depth of luxury wellness platforms. 

The emotional tone balances calm mindfulness with quantifiable momentum. It avoids clinical coldness through softened geometry, rich obsidian undertones, and curated chromatic glows that denote life domains. The visual style relies on refined glass-sheen surfaces, hyper-subtle inner borders, high-legibility typographic hierarchies, and fluid pill silhouettes.

## Colors

The color palette is built over three obsidian foundation steps to establish deep visual layering:
- **Base Canvas:** `#0B0F17` (Deep Obsidian)
- **Layer 1 Surface (Cards & Modules):** `#131A26` (Midnight Slate)
- **Layer 2 Raised Surface (Modals, Overlays, Floating Chips):** `#1A2234` (Elevated Slate)
- **Structural Stroke / Divider:** `#243048` (Crisp Slate Rim)
- **Subtle Outline (Inactive/Ghost):** `#1E283C`

### Domain Accent Pillars
Each functional lifestyle pillar receives a defined paired chromatic identity used for key statistics, progress tracks, and ambient badges:
- **Rest & Sleep:** Moon Violet `#818CF8` (Core), `#6366F1` (Deep Gradient Stop)
- **Habits & Vitals:** Fresh Sage `#10B981` (Core), `#34D399` (Highlight)
- **Wealth & Expenses:** Warm Amber `#F59E0B` (Core), `#FBBF24` (Highlight)
- **Intellect & Reading:** Electric Cyan `#06B6D4` (Core), `#38BDF8` (Highlight)

### Typography & Content Tone
- **Primary Text:** `#F8FAFC` (100% white-slate)
- **Secondary / Label:** `#94A3B8` (Slate 400)
- **Muted / Trackers:** `#64748B` (Slate 500)

## Typography

The typography centers on Plus Jakarta Sans across all operational layers, taking advantage of its rounded terminals and geometric balance to avoid visual strain in low-light environments. 

- **Data & Numerical Values:** Numerals utilize tabular figures (`tnum`) with medium to bold weights to preserve column alignment inside charts, streaks, and expenditure balance summaries.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Upper labels (`label-sm`) default to uppercase tracking (`0.06em`) in muted tones (`#94A3B8`) to distinguish metadata from core telemetry values.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a rhythmic 8pt scale with a fluid composite grid:
- **Mobile (under 640px):** 4-column layout with `margin-sm` (16px) and `gutter-sm` (12px). Micro-cards span either 2 or 4 columns.
- **Tablet (640px to 1024px):** 8-column layout with `margin` (24px) and `gutter` (20px). Allows parallel habit rings and telemetry feeds side-by-side.
- **Desktop (1024px and up):** 12-column layout capped at a max container width of 1280px with `margin-lg` (48px) and `gutter-lg` (28px).

Vertical spacing between card clusters favors breathing room over density, giving priority to quick scanability.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical-like surface tiering combined with luminescent edge lighting rather than drop shadows:
- **Base Tier (Level 0):** Pure `#0B0F17`. Flat background for global canvases.
- **Surface Tier (Level 1):** `#131A26` with a uniform 1px outer stroke of `#243048`. Shadow consists of an ultra-soft, diffused drop shadow (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)` 0px 8px 24px).
- **Interactive Floating Tier (Level 2):** `#1A2234` with an accented top stroke (`linear-gradient(to right, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08), rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02))`) and a subtle ambient glow tinted by the pillar color at 8% opacity.
- **Micro Overlays & Tooltips (Level 3):** Frosted glass surface using `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, tinted with `#131A26` at 85% opacity and a high-contrast hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The interface embraces a continuous pill geometry (Roundedness Level 3) that softens data-heavy metrics.
- **Primary Buttons & Status Badges:** Fully circular/pill boundaries (`9999px` or `rounded-xl`).
- **Standard Cards & Data Modules:** `rounded-lg` (2rem / 32px) for outer perimeters, creating an organic silhouette.
- **Internal Micro-elements & Nested Inputs:** Proportional `1rem` (16px) rounding to maintain nested radii harmony.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill container filled with Primary Moon Violet (`#818CF8`) or contextual domain accent color. Text is `#0B0F17` (bold), offering high contrast. Hover invokes a radial glow (`0 0 20px rgba(129, 140, 248, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Obsidian surface (`#131A26`), 1px border of `#243048`, text `#F8FAFC`. Active state scales down to `0.98` for tactile feedback.

### Status Pills & Chips
- Ultra-compact, full-pill tags (`padding: 4px 12px`).
- Background matches domain accent at 12% opacity (e.g., `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)` for habits).
- Left-aligned 6px pulsating dot with text rendered in matching accent color at `label-sm`.

### Micro-Cards & Habit Modules
- Self-contained surfaces with `padding: space-md` or `space-lg`.
- Top-right corner hosts an actionable micro-toggle or mini progress dial.
- Border shifts dynamically on completion state: transitioning from neutral `#243048` to a subtle tinted border of the relevant pillar accent.

### Progress Bars & Mini-Charts
- **Tracks:** Height 6px to 8px, background `#1A2234` with `rounded-full` caps.
- **Progress Fill:** Dual-tone gradient (e.g., `#06B6D4` to `#38BDF8` for Reading), with an end-cap pin illumination.
- **Sparklines:** Clean vector strokes (2px thickness) with a low-opacity vertical fill gradient fading to zero at the baseline.

### Input Fields
- Recessed slate background (`#0E141F`), interior border `#243048`, pill or deep soft rounding (`1rem`).
- Placeholder text in `#64748B`. Focus transitions the border to Moon Violet (`#818CF8`) with an inset box-glow.

### Checkboxes & Habit Toggles
- Replaced standard square boxes with tactile circular toggles.
- Unchecked: Inset obsidian circle with `#243048` border.
- Checked: Spring animation fill with semantic domain color, presenting an inner white-slate glyph.
