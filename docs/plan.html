Personal Tracker: Build Checklist

A Flutter app for Android. Every feature we agreed on is listed below, grouped by phase. Ticks are saved in this browser.

Overall progress

Reference

Decisions

  • Dedicated modules plus a custom tracker builder
  • Local SQLite only; manual export/import (JSON + CSV)
  • A purchase is an expense with optional item details
  • One currency, set in Settings
  • Home screen is the Today dashboard
  • Phased MVP rollout
  • Investing: prices updated by hand, the app stays offline
  • Investing: FIFO lots (oldest shares sold first)
  • Investing: short-term if held under 1 year, long-term otherwise
  • Investments are kept apart from expenses and budgets
  • Weekly report: an in-app page plus a Sunday-evening notification
  • Memories: yearly occasions and one-time events (past or future) in one module
  • Memories: photos and videos are copied into the app's own storage
  • Memories: past events may have an approximate date (year or month only)

Not in v1

  • App lock (PIN or fingerprint)
  • Income tracking
  • Multiple currencies
  • Naps
  • Counter and number custom trackers
  • Automatic sleep detection
  • Cloud sync
  • Automatic stock prices from the internet
  • Importing trades from a broker
  • Dividends, stock splits and bonus shares
  • Options, futures and short selling
  • Tax reports
  • Exporting the weekly report as an image or PDF
  • Cloud backup of photos and videos
  • Importing birthdays from contacts
  • Sharing memories with other people
  • Editing or trimming photos and videos

Tech stack

  • State: flutter_riverpod
  • Routing: go_router
  • Database: drift (SQLite)
  • Charts: fl_chart
  • Widget: home_widget
  • Notifications: flutter_local_notifications
  • Photos: image_picker
  • Export: share_plus, file_picker
  • Video: video_player plus a thumbnail package
  • Zip backup: archive
